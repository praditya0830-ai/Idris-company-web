The platform

One requirement.
Five buildable designs.

Each variant is sized and checked, with a physics score and a design score, so the trade-offs are visible at a glance. One is chosen; the others stay saved.

Language in.
Engineering out.

A language model reads the request and explains the result. It never produces a number. Every number comes from a deterministic solver built on a published standard.

ISO 6336ISO 281ISO 286 ISO 2768ISO 898VDI 2230
01

Describe

Loads, speeds, space and materials, in plain words. IDRIS asks when a requirement can be read two ways, and shows what each reading changes.

02

Generate

Gears, shafts, bearings, housing, fasteners and oil are sized together. Variants that cannot be made or assembled are discarded.

03

Analyse

3D stress, natural frequencies against running speed, oil temperature and an automatic design review, on any variant.

04

Decide

The engineer chooses. IDRIS exports the chosen design and never changes it silently. When a check fails it shows the failure, explains the cause, and proposes computed fixes to accept or reject.

Gears, bearings, joints

Tooth bending and contact safety factors, rating life for every bearing, and bolted joints to VDI 2230.

Every result
can be traced.

A method enters IDRIS only after it reproduces a published reference case. Each result records the solver version, its inputs and its assumptions.

Results are screening-level. They support engineering review and testing — they do not replace them.

How is this different from Ansys or Abaqus?
IDRIS automates the workflow via AI and cloud solvers. You describe the requirement instead of building the model by hand, and every number still comes from a deterministic solver tied to a published standard — not from the language model.
Do I need a supercomputer?
No. Simulations run on the IDRIS cloud platform.
